Our Equipment

Advanced Veterinary Technology

We invest in state-of-the-art medical equipment to ensure your pet receives the most accurate diagnoses and the highest standard of care.

Digital X-Ray

High-resolution digital radiography allowing us to examine bones, lungs, and internal organs instantly with minimal radiation.

Diagnostic Ultrasound

Non-invasive, real-time imaging used for safe abdominal evaluations, pregnancy checks, and cardiac monitoring.

In-House Blood Laboratory

Complete blood counts and chemistry panels processed in minutes, crucial for emergency situations and pre-anesthetic screening.

Ultrasonic Dental Unit

Advanced dental scaling and polishing equipment to maintain your pet's oral health and prevent periodontal diseases.
